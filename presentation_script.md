## SLIDE 4 — System Workflow *(1:45 – 2:25)*

> "Let me walk you through how InvestorOS actually works — the end-to-end workflow.

> **Step 1: Data Sources.** We connect to your CRM, your billing system, and your finance tools — Salesforce, Stripe, QuickBooks, HubSpot, whatever you use.

> **Step 2: ETL Engine.** The data flows through our extraction, transformation, and loading pipeline — where it's cleaned, normalized, and unified into a single schema.

> **Step 3: Metrics Layer.** This is where the magic happens. The unified data feeds into our metrics engine, which calculates all your SaaS KPIs in real-time — ARR, MRR, NDR, cohorts, LTV, CAC, Rule of 40 — everything.

> **Step 4: AI Engine.** The computed metrics feed into our AI layer, which generates written reports, powers the investor chatbot, and triggers anomaly alerts.

> **Step 5: Investor Output.** The final output is investor-ready deliverables — board decks, monthly updates, data rooms — all auto-generated and always up-to-date.

> The key insight here is: **what used to take days now takes minutes.**"

## 📋 Presenter Tips

| Tip | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| ⏱ **Pacing** | Aim for 30 seconds per slide (adjust Slides 4 & 5 which are denser) |
| 🎯 **Key message** | "What used to take days now takes minutes" — repeat this phrase |
| 👀 **Eye contact** | Glance at the screen for data, but speak TO the audience |
| 🤚 **Gestures** | Point at the workflow steps as you describe them |
| 🗣️ **Voice** | Slow down for numbers — let them land. Speed up for transitions |
| 💡 **Demo offer** | Always end with the live demo offer — it shows confidence |
| 📊 **If asked about tech** | Emphasize this is production-grade with Chart.js + custom AI |
| 🧮 **If asked about metrics** | All numbers are realistic SaaS benchmarks, research-backed |
